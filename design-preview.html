Scoring & race rules

Only completed pairs earn points: 100 + Perfect 25 / Great 15 / Good 5. Combo bonus adds 5 per step after the first, up to 50 per pair.

Wrong food becomes your new target. Your first food or the same tile keeps your combo. Tap another tile briefly to change your aim. On desktop, you can also hold and release Space to jump.

Race lasts 90 seconds against one Easy bot. Food respawns. Highest score wins; equal scores share a tie.
