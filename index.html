How to play

1 Tap a tile or food to aim.
2 Hold the jump button, then release. On desktop, use Space.
The yellow ring shows your landing spot. Match two identical foods.
A wrong match becomes your new target. Center landings earn bonus points.
Perfect: bounce + dance · Great: dance · Good: surprised · Miss: sad
Each completed pair adds 1 COMBO. A wrong match or Miss resets it to 0.
Picking the first food or landing on the same tile keeps your combo.
Sound pitch rises with your combo. Every 5 combos adds a special chime.
BGM: Neon District Lo-fi. Sound toggles both music and effects.
Each pair: 100 + Perfect 25 / Great 15 / Good 5.
Combo bonus: +5 per step after the first, up to +50 per pair.
Only completed pairs earn points. Highest total score wins.
Tied scores use BEST COMBO. Equal scores and combos are a tie.
