Scoring & race rules

Only completed pairs earn points: 100 + Perfect 25 / Great 15 / Good 5. Combo bonus adds 5 per step after the first, up to 50 per pair.

Wrong food becomes your new target. Your first food or the same tile keeps your combo. On desktop, hold and release Space to jump.

Highest score wins. Ties use best combo; equal scores and best combos share a tie. In Solo’s Rival level, a tie means retry.
